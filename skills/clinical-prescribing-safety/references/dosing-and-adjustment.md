# Dosing and Adjustment

Never finalize a dose without doing the arithmetic explicitly.

## Dose types

- **Fixed** vs **weight-based** (mg/kg) vs **BSA-based** (mg/m²).
- **Loading** dose (reach target fast) vs **maintenance**.
- **Per dose** and **per day**; state both. Cap at the **maximum** dose.
- Route, formulation, concentration, and infusion rate (mg/h or mL/h).

## Renal adjustment

- Estimate renal function: **Cockcroft–Gault** (CrCl) or **CKD-EPI** (eGFR) —
  state which, and use the one the label specifies.
  - CrCl (male) = [(140 − age) × weight(kg)] / (72 × SCr); ×0.85 if female.
- Categories that matter: normal, mild, moderate, severe, dialysis.
- Options: reduce dose, extend interval, or avoid. State which and why.
- Dialysis: is the drug dialyzable? Dosing relative to sessions?

## Hepatic adjustment

- Use Child–Pugh (A/B/C) where the label does.
- High first-pass drugs and hepatically-metabolized drugs need caution.
- Watch for hepatic encephalopathy precipitants and fluid/electrolytes.

## Age and weight extremes

- **Pediatric**: weight (and BSA) based; use pediatric-specific references and
  concentration-dependent max doses. Do not extrapolate adult doses.
- **Older adults**: "start low, go slow"; check Beers criteria; consider
  frailty and life expectancy; deprescribe where appropriate.
- **Obesity**: consider adjusted body weight for some drugs; check lipophilic vs
  hydrophilic behavior.

## Tapering and stopping

- Which drugs need a taper (corticosteroids, benzodiazepines, SSRIs, β-blockers,
  PPIs, opioids) and the schedule.
- Rebound/withdrawal risks and the monitoring plan.

## Verification

- Recompute the arithmetic; do not trust a remembered or AI-recalled number.
- Sanity-check against an independent reference (label/BNF/UpToDate/etc.).
- Rounding rules matter (e.g. do not round below a therapeutic threshold;
  measure volumes where possible).
- Confirm the dose is achievable with an available formulation.
